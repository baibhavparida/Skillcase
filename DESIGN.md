The homepage (`src/pages/home-next.jsx`, components in `src/components/next/`, styles in `src/styles/home-next.css`; built as the `/home-next` preview) has a hero (`HeroSplit.jsx`) with the classic homepage's copy: on desktop one featured role card with the other roles' faces orbiting it on two slow rings (hover or click a face to feature it); on phones and tablets a wheel of role cards under the buttons (top arc only, cards tilted along the circle, each card carrying its city, the top card upright with its role and pay beneath; swipe to spin, tap to bring a card up), and the header's Get Started stays hidden until the hero buttons scroll away (`SiteHeader` `revealCta`). (`RoleStrips.jsx`: compact navy-to-photo cards on phones, short expanding photo strips on desktop), "How it works" with animated UI scenes built in code (`StepScenes.jsx`: pointer tap and advisor toast, level bar and flipping medical-German flashcard, offer notification and pay pop-in, plane flying BLR to FRA), on a sticky tinted stage on desktop and an open timeline on phones (no boxes around the steps; the scene's UI card is the only box; tap to replay), a phone-first pay calculator on an edge-to-edge navy band (role and salary first, then the result, then the button; on desktop the column beside it lists what comes with the pay: bonuses and allowances, paid leave and health insurance, no recruitment fees, plus a call-an-advisor link), "Meet our learners" (`LearnersNext.jsx`: the classic homepage's real learners in a slow full-width row, using trimmed or sharper copies of three photos), learner stories as compact video tiles (`PreviewVideo.jsx`) with a Google review wall (four even columns on desktop, the longest review spanning two), the shared app reel (all screens one height; on phones smaller, two in view with a third peeking), a webinar card with a travelling cyan border glow and a tap-to-load podcast (`MediaNext.jsx`), an FAQ with an icon per question (`FaqNext.jsx`) and a short navy closing band with two real app screens in realistic phones tilted in 3D, the front one overlapping the back (`FinalCta.jsx`; `screens` is [back, front], so each page puts its most relevant screen in front). Its blog section is shared with the classic homepage (`HomeSections.jsx`). Patterns came from the owner's reference list and Mobbin, rebuilt in our own style. Reference sites' signature elements (Leap's flag tags, pennant cards, sealed envelope) were removed at the owner's request; don't copy them back in. These pages use Manrope (`BaseLayout` `font="manrope"`; `home-next.css` switches `--font-sans`, matches Poppins' x-height with `font-size-adjust: 0.548` and sets heading tracking to -0.015em); signup, the legal pages, the 404 page and the `-classic` pages stay in Poppins. `/start-now` (`src/pages/start-now-next.jsx`, `start-now-next.css`), linked in the nav as "Learner Stories", is in the same style: the homepage's hero with a plain collage of real learners (name and state on each photo; it replaced the live-class window, which visitors took for something clickable), the story tiles, landscape class and guest-session videos (`PreviewVideo` with `ratio` and `wide`; real frames from the recordings as thumbnails, no shade; each classroom tile has its recording's own shape, the two at one height with column widths in proportion), free notes as a list on a light full-width band, the Google review wall, the app reel, the homepage's FAQ and closing band with the page's own copy. `/about` and `/blog` (with `/blog/<post>`; `about-next.jsx`, `blog-next.jsx`, `blog-next/[slug].jsx`) carry About Us and the blog into the same style (`pages-next.css`): the About hero with its photo, a values bento, "Why we exist" photo tiles, the learners row; a blog built from Stripe, Wise, Babbel and Monzo's patterns: one card format for every guide (photo, category, title, two-line excerpt, date and read time; the whole card is the link, `PostCard.jsx`), the newest guide on a light panel with a "Latest guide" label, and each guide in one reading column (720px at most) with a breadcrumb, byline, what the guide covers, large dark body type and bold section headings; "In this guide" (`GuideContents.jsx`) opens from a row on phones and sits in a sticky rail on wide screens, both marking the section being read; one eligibility prompt halfway through and WhatsApp or copy-link sharing at the end (`ShareGuide.jsx`). Every boxed button on these pages is one height (40px on phones, 44px from 761px; round arrow buttons 40px), set once at the end of `home-next.css`, so buttons side by side always match.

`/signup` (`src/components/CandidateSignup.jsx`) asks the learner app's own onboarding questions, with the exact values the app stores (`src/data/appOnboarding.js`, copied from the app's `OnboardingFlow.jsx`). It ends with a plan for the visitor's German level and goal (`src/components/AppPlanReveal.jsx`, content in `src/data/appPlans.js`, styles in `src/styles/app-plan.css`). The plan shows a realistic phone with the exact app screen that level lands on: navbar, mode tabs, card grid, guided path, job lobby or scholarship hub. It is rebuilt from the live learner app's components (the nursing-german branch), using the app's own images in `public/assets/images/app-ui`. It is drawn at the app's 390 px width and scaled with container units. On phones it rises out of a light-blue band; on desktop it sits in the navy side panel. Option cards are roomy and show one-line descriptions, with a navy border, light tint and check when selected. Signup icons use the site's Phosphor icon font (`src/components/healthcare/Icon.jsx`) rather than icon components, which keeps the page's script small.

## Visual identity

- Manrope on the main pages (`/`, `/start-now`, `/about`, `/blog` and its guides); Poppins on signup, the legal pages, the 404 page and the `-classic` pages. Every page font comes from `--font-sans`, except the signup app-phone preview, which stays Poppins like the real app.
- Navy `#002856`, deep navy `#001f44`, cyan `#05a3e5`, gold `#edb843`.
- Dark blue body text, pale blue-gray surfaces, photography of healthcare professionals.
- Gold primary buttons, navy secondary actions, restrained rounded corners.
- Preserve the alternate Home B page's existing typography and visual identity.

## Layout

Desktop content is centered around an 1180px maximum width. Phone layouts use a single column where reading or forms require it. Horizontal scrollers must expose a visible continuation, preserve access to all items, and support touch and keyboard use.

## Mobile requirements

Prioritize 320–430px phone widths, touch targets of at least 44px, 16px form text, comfortable body copy, safe-area spacing, and persistent access to the main navigation. Desktop layouts remain supported. Use responsive images and defer below-the-fold media.

Recorded from the existing CSS, components, and the owner's confirmed preferences in this task. This documents the existing identity rather than proposing a new brand.
