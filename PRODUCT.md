# Skillcase

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian nurses and healthcare professionals exploring jobs and relocation to Germany. The owner reports that approximately 80% of visitors use mobile phones. Prioritize touch, readable information, variable connection quality, and short browsing sessions.

## Product Purpose

Explain the Germany career pathway, eligibility, language preparation and recruitment support. Help visitors explore roles, read guides, and contact an advisor or complete the existing signup flow.

## Existing Brand

Preserve the approved legacy homepage, healthcare photography, Poppins typography, navy and cyan palette, and gold calls to action. The current copy emphasizes guided, transparent recruitment and practical support.

## Design Principles

- Adapt the existing identity for phones; do not introduce a replacement visual direction.
- Make the next action easy to see and tap.
- Keep all essential information and navigation available on mobile.
- Make long guides comfortable to read and navigate.
- Prioritize useful content over decorative work on slower devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable text, generous touch targets, accessible forms, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and layouts that accommodate narrow screens and zoom. Validate these behaviors; do not imply a formal accessibility certification.

## Scope

Dashboards and login links were removed at the owner's request. Signup currently saves progress locally and ends with advisor contact. This mobile pass does not add backend services or change those product decisions.
