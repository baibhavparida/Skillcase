# Skillcase

## Register

brand

## Users

Indian healthcare professionals exploring jobs and relocation to Germany: nurses (historically the core audience), doctors, physiotherapists, medical lab and radiology technologists, and students starting an Ausbildung. Since October 2026 the brand is positioned for all of these professions, with medical German as the common thread; avoid copy or imagery that reads as nurses-only. The owner reports that approximately 90% of visitors use mobile phones, so design phone-first and treat desktop as the enhancement. Prioritize touch, readable information, variable connection quality, and short browsing sessions.

## Product Purpose

Explain the Germany career pathway for each profession, eligibility, medical German preparation and recruitment support. Help visitors explore roles, read guides, and contact an advisor or complete the existing signup flow.

## Existing Brand

Preserve the approved legacy homepage, healthcare photography, Poppins typography, navy and cyan palette, and gold calls to action. The current copy emphasizes guided, transparent recruitment and practical support.

## Design Principles

- Adapt the existing identity for phones; do not introduce a replacement visual direction.
- Make the next action easy to see and tap.
- Keep all essential information and navigation available on mobile.
- Make long guides comfortable to read and navigate.
- Prioritize useful content over decorative work on slower devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use readable text, generous touch targets, accessible forms, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support, and layouts that accommodate narrow screens and zoom. Validate these behaviors; do not imply a formal accessibility certification.

## Scope

Dashboards and login links were removed at the owner's request. Signup asks the same onboarding questions as the Skillcase learner app and saves the answers on the device, using the app's field names. It ends by showing what the app offers at the visitor's German level, with download links. Answers are not yet sent to the app's backend, and the one-time code is still a placeholder.
